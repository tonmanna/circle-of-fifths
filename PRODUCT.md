# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Guitar and piano learners, often mid-practice with the phone on a stand at arm's length, looking up which chords belong to a key.

## Product Purpose
A circle-of-fifths wheel you spin to pick a key, then see its seven diatonic chords and their notes at a glance.

## Brand Personality
Vintage, classic, refined. Like an engraved sheet-music title page or a well-kept instrument, not a gadget.

## Anti-references
Generic Material apps: default Roboto, stock purple theme, the standard Android look.

## Design Principles
- Readable at arm's length: chord names and keys stay large.
- Musical notation is correct: real ♭ symbols and proper chord spellings.
- The wheel is the interface; everything else supports it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text contrast at least 4.5:1 on the wood surfaces. Swipe has a tap alternative. The wheel has a screen-reader label.
